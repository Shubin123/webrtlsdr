A complete radio receiver in your browser

Plug an RTL-SDR USB stick into your computer or Android phone and listen to FM broadcast, aviation, marine, weather, amateur radio and more. Nothing to install, no account, and no data ever leaves your device.

Capabilities

Plug and play

  • Allow your stick once with Connect SDR; after that it is detected automatically whenever it is plugged in
  • Starts playing on plug-in (can be turned off)
  • Stops cleanly when unplugged and resumes when plugged back in
  • Remembers every setting between visits

Demodulation modes

  • WBFM: FM broadcast with stereo decoding and stereo indicator
  • NBFM: two-way radio, marine, weather, amateur
  • AM: aviation, medium wave, shortwave broadcast
  • USB / LSB: single sideband for amateur and utility HF
  • CW: Morse code
  • Adjustable bandwidth and squelch per mode

Spectrum and waterfall

  • Live spectrum with a frequency scale and auto-levelling noise floor
  • Scrolling colour waterfall
  • Click or drag to tune; scroll to fine-tune
  • The passband is drawn on the display
  • FFT size from 512 to 8192 bins; adjustable display range

Tuning

  • Type a frequency in MHz, kHz, GHz or Hz
  • Step buttons with steps from 10 Hz to 1 MHz, including 6.25, 8.33, 9 and 12.5 kHz channel spacings
  • Retunes the stick only when you leave the visible band, so tuning is instant
  • Keyboard: ← → to step, Shift for ×10, Space to play/stop

Tuner control

  • Automatic gain or manual RF gain from 0 to 50 dB
  • Sample rates from 250 kHz to 2.88 MHz
  • Frequency correction (PPM) for inexpensive crystals
  • Direct sampling (I or Q branch) for HF below 28.8 MHz
  • Bias tee to power an active antenna or LNA
  • Adjustable buffers per second

Bookmarks and recording

  • Bookmark frequencies along with their modes; starter list included
  • Export and import bookmarks as JSON files
  • Record the audio you are hearing and save it to your computer
  • Signal-strength and audio-level meters

Speech to text

  • Live transcript of what you are hearing, with time, frequency and mode for each transmission
  • Runs the Moonshine speech recognition model on your device; audio never leaves your computer
  • Moonshine Base (66 MB, most accurate) or Moonshine Tiny (32 MB, fastest), both more accurate than OpenAI Whisper models of similar size
  • Downloads from this site only when you turn it on, then works offline
  • Splits transmissions automatically; copy or save the transcript as text
  • English only

Private and offline

  • Never contacts any server; all radio processing happens on your device
  • A Content Security Policy blocks every request to any other server
  • No CDN, web fonts, analytics or trackers
  • Works fully offline after the first visit and can be installed as an app

Simulated SDR

  • Try everything without hardware
  • Simulated FM stations near 88.5 MHz (one in stereo), an AM signal on 121.5 MHz, NBFM on 146.52 and 162.55 MHz, AM on 810 kHz and an SSB signal near 14.225 MHz

Requirements

Hardware

  • An RTL2832U-based USB stick with an R820T, R820T2, R828D or R860 tuner (for example, RTL-SDR Blog V3/V4 or a generic DVB-T dongle)
  • An antenna suited to the band you want to hear

Browser

  • Chrome, Edge, Opera or another Chromium-based browser on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS or Android (WebUSB)
  • Firefox and Safari do not support WebUSB

Operating system setup

  • macOS, ChromeOS: nothing to install
  • Android: use a USB OTG adapter
  • Windows: install the WinUSB driver for the stick with Zadig
  • Linux: unload the DVB-T driver (sudo rmmod dvb_usb_rtl28xxu) and give your user access to the device with a udev rule
