        Help & privacy

        Works with RTL2832U-based USB sticks (R820T/R820T2/R828D/R860 tuners) in Chrome, Edge or Opera on desktop and Android.

        First time: plug in the stick and press Connect SDR to give this page permission to use it. After that it is plug and play: the stick is picked up automatically whenever it is plugged in.

        Linux: unload the DVB-T kernel driver first (sudo rmmod dvb_usb_rtl28xxu) and make sure your user can access the USB device. Windows: install the WinUSB driver for the stick with Zadig.

        Shortcuts: Space play/stop, ← → tune by step, Shift+arrows ×10.

        Privacy: this app never contacts any server. All radio processing happens on your computer, settings stay in this browser, and after the first visit it works fully offline. A Content Security Policy blocks requests to any other server. Speech to text runs locally too: the model is served by this site and your audio never leaves your device.
