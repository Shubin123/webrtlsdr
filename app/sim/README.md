`jfk.wav` is an excerpt of President John F. Kennedy's inaugural address
(20 January 1961), a work of the United States government in the public
domain. This 16 kHz mono copy is the sample distributed with whisper.cpp
(SHA-256 59dfb9a4acb36fe2a2affc14bacbee2920ff435cb13cc314a08c13f66ba7860e).
The simulated SDR broadcasts it so speech to text can be tried without
hardware.
